# Vibrance and trunk review

Compare `wide-room.png` with `reference-photo.png`.

## Changed

- Trunks up to about 25 cm thick, as measured from the photo, with grooves that follow the grain.
- Neutral tone mapping replaces AgX, which was muting strong colours. Plants, fish, and background are more saturated, and there is less haze.
- Caustics are softer, the room is dimmer, and depth of field no longer blurs the wide view.

## Still looks wrong

Most serious first.

1. **Trunk surface.** Long smooth ridges look a little like moulded plastic. The photo's wood is rougher and more broken, with moss on it.
2. **Fins.** A fine screen-door grain shows on see-through fins, most of all on the gold angelfish.
3. **Plants.** Vibrant now, but still clean and alike up close.
4. **Rocks and moss.** Rocks are smaller and less prominent than in the photo, and moss is still thin.
5. **Light at the surface.** The photo has a bright white glow and bubble curtain at the top left. Mine is more even, and the bubbles are faint.
6. **Room colour.** The right wall is still teal, which needs a rebake to fix.
