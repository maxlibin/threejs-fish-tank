# Milestone 4 review: tank and room at the photo's scale

Compare `wide-room.png` with `reference-photo.png`.

## Close to the photo

- Tank 3 m x 70 cm x 1.35 m, nearly wall to wall, as measured from the photo.
- Thick sweeping driftwood, a tall bush climbing on the right, open water in the upper middle, sand strip at the front.
- 72 fish: 56 tetras, 2 silver angelfish, 1 gold angelfish, 8 corydoras, 5 otocinclus. They are enlarged beyond real sizes, to match the picture.
- Room: four-door cabinet, hood and LED bar, ceiling track lights, wood panels with LED strip, TV on black console, grey sectional, curtains, all in Cycles-baked light.

## Still looks wrong

Most serious first.

1. **Tank light.** The photo has a bright white-blue glow in the top third and a dark, dramatic room. Mine is flatter and more evenly lit.
2. **Plants up close.** Leaves are clean and repeat in shape. The photo's mix has more species, sizes, and textures.
3. **Moss.** Patchy over rocks; the photo has deeper, softer cushions over rock and wood.
4. **Background.** A little flat and blue. The photo's goes from pale near the surface to dark green-grey below.
5. **Wall colour.** The right wall picks up too much teal, and the wood is more orange than the photo's grey-brown.
6. **Fish at 25 cm.** Still read as illustrations.
7. **TV picture.** A static image.

## Frame rate

Failing, at 21-24 fps on average in the last full run, with the owner's Brave tab also running the app and sharing the GPU. Needs re-measuring with only one copy running.
