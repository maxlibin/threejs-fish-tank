## Still looks wrong

Most serious first.

1. **Fish seen from the front or back.** Skin is projected from the side, so a fish turning toward you looks thin and stretched.
2. **Both sides of a fish are the same**, and all tetras share one photo, so a close look at a school shows copies.
3. **The gold angelfish** is a recoloured silver one, so it keeps darker bars that the photo's orange fish lacks.
4. **Catfish** still use the older painted skin.
5. **Plant species.** Land plants stand in for aquatic ones.
6. **Room.** The sofa has few real creases, and the TV is a still image.
