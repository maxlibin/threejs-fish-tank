# School variety and catfish review

Compare `wide-room.png` and `angelfish-closeup.png` with `reference-photo.png`.

## Changed

- Mid-water fish swim along the tank and show their sides more than 90% of the time, checked by a test.
- The school uses three skins from two photos. Corydoras and otocinclus wear real photos too.
- The gold angelfish's dark bars are muted, and tetra fins read as nearly clear.

- Wood, rocks, and plants are all kept inside the glass and under the water, checked by a test on every vertex.

## Still looks wrong

Most serious first.

1. **Both sides of a fish are the same**, and close looks still reveal repeated skins.
2. **Fins** are opaque colours tinted like the water behind them, so plants do not show through them.
3. **Plant species.** Land plants stand in for aquatic ones.
4. **Wood.** After fitting the branches inside the glass, there is less gnarled wood showing than in the photo.
5. **Close-up leaves** look too bright and glowing.
6. **Room.** The sofa has few real creases, and the TV is a still image.
